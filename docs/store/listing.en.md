# YouTube Comment Block · Pause Before Comments

## Short description

Hide YouTube comments until you choose to see them. Pause with a reflection prompt or research insight before opening comments.

## Detailed description — copy below

Video first. Your thoughts first.

YouTube Comment Block hides comments on YouTube video pages until you explicitly choose to see them.

If you find yourself opening comments out of habit, or checking everyone else’s reaction before forming your own opinion, a short question in the comment area gives you a moment to decide.

HOW IT WORKS
• The comment area asks: “Do you really want to see the comments?”
• Choose “Focus on the video” to leave comments hidden.
• Choose “Yes, show comments” to reveal comments for the current video only.
• Use “Hide again” whenever you want to close them.
• A new video asks for a fresh choice. Consent does not carry across videos.

A LITTLE ROOM TO REFLECT
Before opening comments, you see one of four short prompts: a reflection question or an insight from research on social influence and uncivil comments. Research insights include source links, context, and limitations. They do not claim that every comment is harmful or that the extension produces a proven health benefit.

AT HOME IN YOUTUBE
The choice appears where comments normally live, without covering the video. It follows YouTube’s light and dark themes, supports the comments side panel, and provides Korean and English text.

LOCAL AND SIMPLE
No extension account, ads, subscription, or analytics. The current video ID and the comment area’s page structure are processed temporarily on your device to control visibility. Comment text and browser history are not extracted into records or stored. No user data is sent to developer servers. Research links open externally only when you click them.

SUPPORTED SCOPE
Designed for regular video pages on www.youtube.com in desktop Chrome. YouTube Shorts, live chat, the mobile website, and the YouTube mobile app are not supported. The extension hides comments visually; it does not block YouTube from downloading them. Refresh existing YouTube tabs after installation.

This is an independent extension and is not made, affiliated with, or endorsed by YouTube or Google. Research information is educational and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or guaranteed health outcomes.
